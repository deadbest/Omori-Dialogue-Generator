How to use? (show more)
  1. Select the desired settings below
  2. Edit the contents of each of the boxes
    • Character name is simple enough! Just click on it and edit.
    • Click on the portrait space to upload your custom portrait
    • Click on the background area to upload your custom background or use the 'Select Background' button to choose from the options available.
    • Ensure your dialogue takes up no more than three lines to ensure it renders properly.
  3. Press the "download" button to download your dialogue!
